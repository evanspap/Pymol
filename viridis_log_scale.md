To focus your **viridis** color scale on small values instead of distributing it linearly from 0 to 40, you should use a ***logarithmic scale function** or a **power-law/square root scale function*** before mapping the numbers to your color palette.

Here are the best functions to achieve this depending on your plotting environment:

1\. The Logarithmic Scale (Most Dramatic Focus)

A log scale stretches the color transitions dramatically across the smallest numbers and compresses them heavily for larger values.
